# What changed from content-atomizer 4.1.1, and why

| Original | sergical version | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asks which 2 or 3 of 8 platforms the brand runs | Fixed: X, YouTube, LinkedIn; everything else skipped | He only runs those three. |
| "YouTube long-form is not an atomization target, route to /creative" | YouTube gets an angle: hook, title direction, search evidence | YouTube is one of his three channels; packaging itself goes through his DevEx app, where he picks the title. |
| Platform-fit table for generic content shapes | Fit table for his kinds of sources: tools he built, systems with rules, livestreams, opinions | Every source he atomizes is one of these. |
| Livestreams treated like any video source | Streams stay on Sentry's channel; X gets a clip that opens on the payoff | His channel copies get 5 to 104 views vs 4 to 8x on Sentry's; his event-title posts are his bottom 5. |
| X links default to a first reply (30 to 50% penalty) | Links allowed in the post; the post carries the value without the click | X said in 2026 that links no longer deboost; his bottom 5 are bare links, not posts with links. |
| Calendar mode with `recommended_post_time` and Zernio/Buffer queueing | Removed | Drafts only; he posts himself. |
| No private-source handling | Phase 2 marks each atom public / ratio-only / private and drops private ones | He builds in public from private repos (for example his fleet inventory). |
| Quality gate | Kept, plus hard fails: unsourced claim, private detail, bare link, Sentry ad | His rules. |
| Engagement-replies mode | Removed for now | `sergical-social-content` covers replies in QT/REPLY mode. |
